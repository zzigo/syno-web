# Historical procedures in syNo

These are original, short procedural studies, explicitly labelled **After Studie I** and **After Studie II**. They do not reproduce Stockhausen's full works, their published score, or recorded masters. The new schedules, amplitudes and form are generated by `scripts/build-historical-studies.py`; no external Csound/Max source or score table is redistributed or relicensed as syNo code.

## After Studie I — 42 seconds

Primary reference: [Stockhausen's programme note, reproduced by IRCAM](https://brahms.ircam.fr/en/works/work/12155). It describes composing spectra through the number of sinusoidal components, their individual frequency/amplitude, and their individual lifetimes and entrances/exits.

The syNo miniature implements that additive approach with twelve newly composed spectra (44 pure sine events), per-component envelopes, staggered entrances and a mono centre image. It is a procedural homage, not a claim to reconstruct the unpublished/unsupplied full score. No FM, carrier synthesis or imported recording stands in for those individual components.

## After Studie II — 37.05 seconds

Primary construction source: [Antonio de Sousa Dias's instrument-simulation repository](https://github.com/asousadias/Stockhausen_ElektronischStudie2_1954), especially its README, `studie IIb.orc` and `read-me_more(FR).txt`. The repository explicitly presents an **excerpt** of Studie II and two realization approaches. Its second approach emphasizes short sequential sine slices entering a reverberation chamber, with the output observed after the initial excitation rather than hearing dry tones plus a decorative reverb.

The syNo version independently derives frequencies from `100 * 5^(k/25)`. Each object has five equally spaced lattice indices; spacings 1–5 create five mixture families. The fifteen-object temporal order and level trajectories are original, not converted from the repository's score excerpt.

Each mixture excites one convolution bus with five consecutive 55.5 ms slices. All excitation ends by 277.5 ms. A following output envelope remains zero for at least 350 ms (500/700 ms in the longer objects), so none of the dry fragments reaches the listener. Only the reverberated material is then admitted. Written bus order is essential: `r9v0>0>peak>tail>0'duration`.

This demonstrates the construction principle with current syNo tools. It uses syNo's existing synthetic stereo 6.65-second r9 impulse, not a measured mono chamber or the 10-second Csound reverberator. The output contours and original object form differ from the historical work. A separate source-backed translation of the supplied Hajdu Max score table is now available as Studie II · Hajdu → syNo; see [translation and sonic differences](hajdu-studie-ii.md). The After Studies remain independent original miniatures.

## Reich · future

Moved from Sequences to Pieces without changing its note material or rotating-cell mechanism. The preset deliberately keeps **Loop ON**, unlike finite Pieces: the future mutation is evaluated across successive transport cycles. This is the existing synthetic rotating-cell realization, not an acoustic-piano sample or a new transcription.

After Studie II now stores its 81 derived absolute frequencies once in a u[f ...] table and passes positions to named mixture functions; its original event frequencies, timings and shape are preserved.
